# SOURCES — claude-liam-the-smallest-lever

Companion to `claude-liam-the-lever-nobody-pulled`. That reel establishes that the operator
layer exists and is unused. This one asks what should go in it. Watch order matters: this
film assumes the layer is understood and never re-explains it.

Fact-check performed 2026-08-03.

## What this reel is sourced from, and what that means

Most of this film is **argument, not evidence**, and it is important to be clear about which
beats are which — because the failure mode for a design film is dressing up a preference as a
finding.

**Evidence beats** — these make factual claims about the world and are sourced:

- **B07** — Anthropic and OpenAI both ship a user-facing study/learning mode. Vendor
  documentation; carried from the research document's Part 2 rather than re-read here. The
  beat's actual claim is a question ("is it on?"), which is safe under either reading.
- **B11 / B12** — Washington University's public guidance for building a Socratic tutor tells
  staff to test it adversarially, and names two specific probes: does the bot cave if a
  student begs, and does it fold if the student introduces a fake term. From the research
  document's Part 5 inventory. This is the reel's one piece of praise, and praise is a
  low-risk attribution.
- **B13** — Utah operates a review-and-publish pipeline requiring owner identity, audience,
  data disclosures and a maintenance plan before a custom assistant goes live, and has a named
  office with a chief AI officer. Duke's MyGPT Builder provisions dedicated instances per
  class or department with admin control over models, knowledge and tools. Both from Part 5.
  Neither has a publicly named live deployment, and the beat says exactly that.

**Argument beats** — B01 through B10, B14 through B17. These are reasoning from the
enforcement gap in Part 3 and the pedagogical-shape brainstorm, and they are the author's
position, not a survey result. The reel presents them as reasoning: B01 kills a proposal, B02
gives a mechanism, B04–B07 propose, B09 attacks all four proposals, B15 filters them. Nothing
in that sequence is stated as a finding about what institutions have done.

## The four axes, and where they come from

Scope-not-manner, disclosure-not-restriction, assignment-grain-not-course-grain, and
visible-switch-not-silent-shift are the source document's own four shapes. The reel keeps
them in the source's order and adds one thing the source did not: a **single filter** (B15)
that ranks them by what they assume rather than by what they achieve. Two survive it.

That filter is the reel's contribution and it is also its most attackable claim. It is stated
as a consequence of the reframe in B14 — if you accept "assume you can enforce nothing," then
assignment-gating and the switch fall out by construction. A viewer who rejects the reframe
is entitled to keep all four, and B14 draws the reframe as a Transform rather than a
strike-through specifically so that the earlier question is visibly *grown up*, not dismissed.

## Quarantined material — carried over from the companion reel

A second research pass on this question fabricated a checkable detail about the co-authors of
the paper it was assisting. Everything specific it produced is quarantined: Harvard's "AI
Sandbox," a Canvas-integrated "PingPong"/"Coaching Bot," Boston University's "TerrierGPT,"
"50,000 students across 13 campuses," "3,500 Maizey instances."

**None of it appears in this reel.** The companion film argues about that pass explicitly in
its B17; this film simply does not use its output.

## Naming rules

Institutions are named. Individuals are not — not the WashU author of the guidance, not the
Utah chief AI officer, not the York instructor who appears in the companion reel, and not
anyone in the correspondence that prompted the research. The private committee thread behind
this research line is not referenced, quoted, characterised or alluded to anywhere.

## The one deliberate provocation, and its counterweight

B14 calls a research question that assumes enforcement "policy theatre." That is a strong
line about work real people are doing, and it is the strongest editorial claim in the film.

It is earned in two places and both must survive the edit. **B09** attacks all four of the
reel's own proposals before it attacks anyone else's, so the film is not standing outside the
problem throwing rocks. **B13** explicitly refuses to treat governance-without-a-published-
artifact as a failure — the production note on that beat says the gap is in the evidence, not
in the institution. Cut either and B14 reads as contempt rather than as an argument.

## Register note

Teardown, but constructive rather than diagnostic — the companion reel finds a gap, this one
proposes what goes in it and then tries to break its own proposals. The verdict is a
narrowing, not a win: two of four controls survive, and they survive by being less ambitious
than the ones that failed.
