## A Note on Sources

This guide keeps citations light in the body text in order to remain practical and readable. It draws on a small set of well-established frameworks, and the references below identify sources for readers who want to explore the research further. Full research notes, with additional sources and verification flags, are maintained alongside the course materials.

***

## References

**Mentorship frameworks and evidence**

- Association of American Medical Colleges. (2017). *Compact Between Biomedical Graduate Students and Their Research Advisors.* AAMC. — The model on which the Mentorship Compact (Module 1) is based.
- National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine. (2019). *The Science of Effective Mentorship in STEMM.* National Academies Press. — Evidence base for inclusive, effective mentorship (Modules 3 and 5).
- Pfund, C., Branchaw, J., & Handelsman, J. *Entering Mentoring* / the CIMER curriculum, including Culturally Aware Mentoring. — Practical mentor-competency frameworks (Modules 1, 3, 5).

**Scaffolding and skill development (Module 2, Module 6)**

- Collins, A., Brown, J. S., & Newman, S. E. (1989). Cognitive apprenticeship: Teaching the crafts of reading, writing, and mathematics. In *Knowing, Learning, and Instruction.* — Modeling → Scaffolding → Fading.
- Wood, D., Bruner, J. S., & Ross, G. (1976). The role of tutoring in problem solving. *Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry, 17*(2), 89–100. — Origin of "scaffolding."
- Vygotsky, L. S. (1978). *Mind in Society.* Harvard University Press. — The Zone of Proximal Development.
- Deci, E. L., & Ryan, R. M. Self-Determination Theory (autonomy support). — Foundation for trust calibration and fostering independence (Module 6).

**Psychological safety, identity, and a culture of honest reporting (Module 3, Module 5)**

- Edmondson, A. C. (1999). Psychological safety and learning behavior in work teams. *Administrative Science Quarterly, 44*(2), 350–383.
- Carlone, H. B., & Johnson, A. (2007). Understanding the science experiences of successful women of color: Science identity as an analytic lens. *Journal of Research in Science Teaching, 44*(8), 1187–1218.
- Estrada, M., et al. Models of integration into the scientific community and the role of mentorship in persistence. — Belonging and science identity.
- On a "just culture" of blameless error reporting: Reason, J. (1997), *Managing the Risks of Organizational Accidents*; Dekker, S. (2007), *Just Culture.* — Basis for the No-Fault Error Policy.

**Documentation, data integrity, and career planning (Module 1, Module 4)**

- ALCOA+ data-integrity principles and the FAIR data principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable). — Standards behind ELN documentation and the handover checklist.
- Individual Development Plan (IDP) frameworks, including *myIDP* (Science Careers / AAAS). — Structure for the Module 4 career-trajectory conversation.
